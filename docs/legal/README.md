# Legal documents of the hosted platform

The three documents here are the hosted platform's terms of service, data processing agreement and privacy policy. They
are built on Common Paper's standard terms under CC BY 4.0, reviewed by counsel and published at
`https://ncodeid.com/legal`, and Git history is their record of changes
([ADR-1529](../decisions/1529-the-hosted-platforms-terms-dpa-and-privacy-policy-are-public-documents-built-on-common-papers-standard-terms.md)).

- [`terms-of-service.md`](./terms-of-service.md) is the cover page and order form that incorporate Common Paper's Cloud
  Service Agreement Standard Terms.
- [`data-processing-agreement.md`](./data-processing-agreement.md) is the cover page that incorporates Common Paper's
  DPA Standard Terms for the personal data the platform processes on a customer's behalf.
- [`privacy-policy.md`](./privacy-policy.md) says what personal data NCode Group collects as the controller, why, and
  what rights people have over it.
