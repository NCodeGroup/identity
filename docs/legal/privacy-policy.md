# Privacy Policy

- **Status:** Draft

NCode Group is the controller of the personal data this policy describes, and the policy takes effect when it is
published at `https://ncodeid.com/legal/privacy`
([ADR-1529](../decisions/1529-the-hosted-platforms-terms-dpa-and-privacy-policy-are-public-documents-built-on-common-papers-standard-terms.md)).
It doesn't cover the end users of a customer's tenants, whose data NCode Group processes for the customer under the
[Data Processing Agreement](data-processing-agreement.md).

## What we collect

- Account holders' name, email address and organization.
- Billing details, which the payment processor collects and keeps.
- Support correspondence.
- The request logs of the management console and the website: IP address, user agent and time.

We collect no advertising identifiers.

## Why we use it

We use personal data to provide and secure the service, bill for it, answer support requests and meet legal
obligations. Under the GDPR the legal bases are the contract with the account holder, our legitimate interests in
security, and the legal obligation.

## Cookies

The management console sets one strictly necessary `__Host-` session cookie. We set no analytics or advertising
cookies.

## Who receives it

The subprocessors listed in Annex 2 of the [Data Processing Agreement](data-processing-agreement.md) and the payment
processor receive the personal data they need to do their work. We don't sell or share personal information as the CCPA
defines those terms.

## Where it is kept

We keep personal data in the United States.

## How long we keep it

- Account data: for the life of the account and 30 days after.
- Logs: 30 days.
- Billing records: as long as tax law requires.

## Your rights

You may ask for access to your personal data, its correction, deletion or portability, and may object to or ask us to
restrict its use. You may also complain to a supervisory authority. California and other US state residents have the
rights their state's privacy law gives them. Send a request to `privacy@ncodeid.com`, and we answer within 30 days.

## Children

The service is not directed to children under 16.

## Changes

A change is published here with a new effective date, and this file's history in Git is the history of the policy.
